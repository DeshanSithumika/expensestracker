Expense Tracker

Track spending privately, share costs with friends

Expense Tracker keeps your money records on your phone. There is no account to create, no server, no ads and no analytics.

What it does

  • Record expenses, income and transfers across cash, bank and card accounts.
  • Monthly budgets per category, with alerts at 80% and 100%.
  • Reports by category and month.
  • Groups: split trips, flats and events equally, by amounts or by percent, and see who owes whom.
  • Scan receipts on the phone.
  • Optional on Android: spot payments in bank notifications and offer to add them. Nothing is added without your review.
  • Encrypted backups to your files or, optionally, your own Google Drive.

Your data

Everything stays on your phone unless you make a backup. Backups are encrypted on the phone with a passphrase only you know. Read the privacy policy.
